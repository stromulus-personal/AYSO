## Game format (7v7 / U9)

- 7v7, size 4 ball
- Build-out line required; opponents retreat on goal kicks & GK possession
- Offside judged at the build-out line, not midfield
- No punting or drop kicks by the keeper
- No headers
- 10U technical areas **closed and controlled** this year — no exceptions

## Season plan (block structure, not week-by-week)

| Weeks | Block | Mantra |
|-------|-------|--------|
| 1–3 | Ball mastery | "the ball is my friend" |
| 4–6 | 1v1 courage | "go at them" (praise the attempt, not the outcome) |
| 7–9 | Finding a friend | "look up, then decide" (dribble or pass as a CHOICE) |
| 10–12 | Playing our shape | "spread out and support," use the build-out line deliberately |

**Threads all season:** one team value per month; rotating captains; everyone plays every position including keeper; same closing huddle ritual.

> Note: weeks 6–8 is when win/loss talk leaks in. Have the language ready **first**.

## Practice

- Small games over drills; 300+ touches per kid; scrimmage every session
- Rotate activities every 10–15 min
- **Name rondo** first two practices (name a player, pass to them)
  - Progressions: add a defender / call the name you're passing AWAY from / two balls
  - Reuse it later when a new kid joins — makes the arrival a team activity

## Add-ons (region-provided, free)

- **Challenger program** — international youth semi-pros running sessions
- **First touch clinic** for new players

Both separate from my practices. Free + regional means push for full turnout:

- Get dates/locations before leaving
- Frame as a team thing, not an optional extra
- Sort carpools early
- Steal their warm-up games

## Sportsmanship coin

- Awarded by coach, by ref, or to whole team — **confirm which version is ours**
- Encouraged that EVERY kid gets one
- Meaning comes from specificity, not scarcity: name the actual act, not "great sportsmanship"
- Bank observations early, especially for the difficult kid and for Isaac (coach's kid usually gets short-changed by overcorrection)
